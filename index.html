Credits & data sources

Road and rail centrelines come from OpenStreetMap contributors, queried live through the Overpass API and licensed under the ODbL. They are what keeps the car on the street and the train on the rails; when Overpass is unreachable the sim falls back to free driving and the built-in station chain.

The road car is Car Concept from the Khronos glTF Sample Assets, © 2021 Khronos Group, licensed CC-BY 4.0, itself derived from a public-domain model by Unity Fan.

About the sign-in

Any of the accounts in AUTH_ACCOUNTS can sign in — there's an owner login and a shared guest login for friends. Credentials are checked as salted SHA-256 digests, so no password is readable in the page source. This keeps it off a casual reader's screen — it isn't real access control, because everything here runs in your browser and a determined person with developer tools can step around it. Anything that must actually be protected needs a server.
