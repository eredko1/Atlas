About the sign-in

Any of the accounts in AUTH_ACCOUNTS can sign in — there's an owner login and a shared guest login for friends. Credentials are checked as salted SHA-256 digests, so no password is readable in the page source. This keeps it off a casual reader's screen — it isn't real access control, because everything here runs in your browser and a determined person with developer tools can step around it. Anything that must actually be protected needs a server.
